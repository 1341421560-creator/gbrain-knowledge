Innovative Materials

Aramid Fiber
Aramid fiber, with high tensile strength and low density, is widely used in making high-end composite products that are strong yet light, such as bullet-proof vests, spacecraft parts, bunker suits, and cables.

Carbon Fiber
Carbon fiber is lighter and stronger than almost all kinds of metal. It's used to make the body and wings of airplanes, small components, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), sports cars, etc.

Using high-tech materials including aramid fiber, carbon fiber, and other composites, make PITAKA products slim yet strong, comfortable and fashionable.
